WritingProof Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

WritingProof is a Microsoft Word add-in that tracks writing sessions to help users prove their documents were written by a human. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

  • Writing session data: timestamps, character counts, typing and paste statistics
  • Document metadata: document title

How We Use Your Data

  • All session data is stored locally in your browser's local storage within the add-in
  • Proof reports are exported as JSON files to your local device
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers
  • We do not read or store the actual content of your documents
  • We do not share your data with any third parties

Data Storage

All WritingProof data is stored exclusively on your local device. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in the add-in.

Data Protection Mechanisms

  • Local storage only: All session data remains on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Cryptographic integrity: All proof reports are protected with SHA-256 cryptographic hash chains, ensuring data cannot be tampered with without detection.
  • Minimal data collection: WritingProof only collects writing metadata. The actual content of your documents is never read or stored.
  • No third-party sharing: Your data is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third parties.

Permissions

WritingProof requests the following permissions:

  • ReadWriteDocument: To monitor document changes for writing session tracking
