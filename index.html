WritingProof

Prove your writing is human. Fight back against false AI detection.

A Google Docs™ add-on that tracks your writing sessions and generates a tamper-proof cryptographic report — so you can prove every word was written by you.

Why WritingProof?

AI detection tools like Turnitin are generating false positives — flagging genuine human-written work as AI-generated. Students and researchers are facing serious academic consequences for work they actually wrote themselves.

WritingProof gives you the evidence to fight back.

How It Works

📝 Automatically tracks every writing session with timestamps
⌨️ Detects typing vs copy-paste events
🔐 Generates a SHA-256 cryptographic proof chain
📊 Creates a detailed report with writing speed and session history
📱 Generates a QR code certificate for your thesis or paper
☁️ All data stored in your personal Google Drive — never shared

Getting Started

Step 1: Install WritingProof from Google Workspace Marketplace™
Step 2: Open any Google Docs™ document — click WritingProof → Open WritingProof Panel from the menu to open the sidebar.
Step 3: In the sidebar, click Start Tracking to begin recording your writing session.
Step 4: Write your document normally. The sidebar tracks your typing and paste events in real-time every 10 seconds.
Step 5: Keep the sidebar open while writing — tracking pauses if the sidebar is closed.
Step 6: Click Stop Tracking in the sidebar when finished. Your session is saved to Google Drive™ automatically.
Step 7: Click Show Report to see your writing statistics, or Generate QR Code to create a certificate for your document.
