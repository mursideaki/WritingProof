WritingProof Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Overview

WritingProof is a Google Docs add-on that tracks writing sessions to help users prove their documents were written by a human. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

  • Your Google account email address (used as author identification in reports)
  • Writing session data: timestamps, character counts, typing and paste statistics
  • Document metadata: document title and ID

How We Use Your Data

  • All data is stored locally in your Google Document's properties
  • Session reports are saved to your personal Google Drive
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers
  • We do not read or store the actual content of your documents
  • We do not share your data with any third parties

Data Storage

All WritingProof data is stored exclusively in:

  • Your Google Document's properties (session tracking data)
  • Your Google Drive (proof reports in JSON format)

You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in the add-on menu.

Permissions

WritingProof requests the following permissions:

  • Google Docs: To monitor document changes for writing session tracking
  • Google Drive: To save proof reports to your personal Drive
  • Email: To identify you as the document author in reports
  • Script Storage: To save session data within your document
